# First MAJOR build: the Backyard Burner platform world
Date: 2026-11-11
Status: AUTHORIZED, IN_PROGRESS

## Four delivery passes
1. Playable hub, embodied equipment operation, iconographic onboarding and three distinct jobs.
2. Review-led polish of two levels while preserving three playable scenarios; saves, UI, narrative skeleton, biome modifiers and preset competent gear.
3. Campaign of 7+ jobs, economy/progression, equipment access, richer biomes, adaptive music and cutscenes.
4. Complete story/challenge/puzzle modes, character/ability variety, secrets and multiple endings.

The existing miniature hub and flame/sound engineering slice do not satisfy the visual and spatial scope of Pass One. The user supplied current screenshots and requested a taller platformer, hub area and higher-resolution pixel art. They are evidence of the current crude UI, not reference art to copy.

## Major-build deliverable
A portrait-first industrial adventure with a world at least two viewport heights tall, four reachable levels, real ladders/jumping/gravity, following camera, detailed editable industrial scenery and an animated PPE operator. The Backyard Burner becomes a home/workshop/contract hub with upper decks, equipment bench, home burner and a simple operator interaction. Three jobs use distinct industrial locations. Mobile controls are compact, usable and inside one screen; the scene gets most of the space.

Art direction remains industrial pixel art. The native world viewport moves from 640x360 to a default 768x1152, with finer authored texture, shading and character detail. Merely scaling existing rectangles or increasing canvas dimensions is not acceptance. The studio chooses composition, palettes, scenery and visual personality within the shared contract.

## Production departments and dependencies
1. Environment/animation specialist authors game/world/industrial-yard.mjs: coherent level data, hub, scenes, layered pixel art and operator animation.
2. Gameplay specialist consumes that contract and authors game/player/platformer.mjs: collisions, jump, ladders, camera and proximity.
3. Integration specialist connects both to the actual game in main.mjs/index.html/style.css, preserving the causal burner/flame/sound and three jobs.
4. Independent source review, trusted behavioral checks, actual mobile Chrome and visual inspection gate integration and preview publication. The technical operator advances the complete finite batch; the user is not asked to launch each job.

Each work order has its own bounded reservation under the existing $2/run, $5/day and $50 cumulative policy. No schedule, unbounded retries or changes to the separate BoRHeZ Games Studio are authorized by this build. The last working preview remains available until this major candidate passes its combined checks.
