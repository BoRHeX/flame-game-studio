Operating guide

Drag the thumb stick to walk or climb ladders. Jump and interact with the opposite thumb. Use the contract board in the workshop to begin.

Each job follows ◎ inspect → ⚒ repair → ↻ adjust → ◉ verify → ✓ hold. Walk up to equipment and interact. Rotate fuel wheels, drag air levers, or use the slider and detent buttons.

Backyard: secure the intake shield on the first catwalk, then balance main air. Warehouse: free the draft linkage on the top catwalk and test it beyond the old travel stop. Heat-Hold: balance heat, soot and stability.

Observe at the chamber port. Watch mode accelerates the process and deadline while reducing cash drain. The menu and save slots pause the game. Keyboard: WASD/arrows, Space jump, E interact, Escape close.
